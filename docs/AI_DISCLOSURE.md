# AI assistance disclosure

OpenAI dot/Codex assisted with product design, interface design, implementation, deterministic evidence rules, test development, debugging, documentation, and demonstration preparation. The participant directed the project and remains responsible for its submission, claims, security, and compliance.

The application itself does not call a language model. Its scores and classifications come from inspectable deterministic JavaScript rules. Live discovery and corroboration use SerpApi's Google Search and Google Jobs APIs.

Example-mode organizations, opportunities, rates, dates, and excerpts are fictional fixtures authored for demonstrating edge cases. They are explicitly labeled and are not represented as real search results. No existing private user code, emails, contact lists, or private opportunity information is included.
